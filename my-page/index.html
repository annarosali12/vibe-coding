The Origin Story 📖✨

How a small puppy became the reigning social butterfly

My mom officially proclaimed me the most hyperextroverted dog she has ever met—and honestly, she's completely spot on! From the second I wake up, my tail starts wagging, and my mission is clear: spread love, claim belly rubs, and turn every room into a celebration.

My mom affectionately calls me her "party girl" because if there is a gather, a barbecue, a birthday party, or even two humans conversing on a sidewalk, I am going to trot right into the middle of the fun.

I genuinely believe that there are no strangers in this world—only friends I haven't showered with kisses yet! Wherever I step paw, a party instantly follows.

My Secret Hobby: Target Training! 🎯✨

Working hard for those party trick applause moments!

When I'm not social-butterflying my way across the neighborhood, my absolute favorite hobby is Clicker Target Training with my mom! Hearing that sharp CLICK! followed by a delicious cheese treat is pure magic to my ears.

I touch target sticks with my nose, place my paws on target mats, and learn fancy new choreography in no time flat. It keeps my clever brain sharp and lets me build up an incredible repertoire of crowd-pleasing party tricks to show off to all my guests!
