Audio & Scene Description

The world is center of a meadow in Sweden. The grass is long and green. Cows graze on one side of a fence. They stare at you as you walk by, huffing and chewing on blades of grass. The path you follow is a double line of gray, left after many tractors and cars passed through. Farmhouses are red with white trim. The path slopes down through a forest. It becomes gravel, then simply flattened grass after hundreds of footprints. It ends at a pair of flat rocks, ideal for having a picnic. Beyond them is a deep, cool lake surrounded by pine trees. The water is clean, turned a copper hue from the pine needles that have dropped into it. It’s perfect for swimming.
