Sidewalk Whiskers

Take a relaxing stroll down Maple Avenue and care for the neighborhood's stray and friendly cats.

  • Walk the sidewalk: Use Left/Right arrows, A/D keys, the Walk buttons, or tap anywhere on the sidewalk.
  • Feed approaching cats: Press Feed a Cat to lean down and set a food bowl. When multiple cats approach at once, you automatically scoop and serve faster!
  • Pet happy cats: Press Pet a Cat to kneel down—cats will rub their cheeks on your hand or weave happily around your feet.
  • Watch their tails: Tail up means eager, tail down means shy, and a twitching tail means curious!
