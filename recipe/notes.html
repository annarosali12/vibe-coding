About This Recipe

The story behind the mac & cheese

Notes

This recipe started as a way to use up whatever cheese and pasta shapes were on hand, and it's been tweaked over the years until the ratios felt right. Shredding the cheddar yourself, rather than buying it pre-shredded, makes a noticeably smoother sauce.

The base is a simple roux: butter and flour cooked together before the milk goes in. It looks like an extra step, but it's what keeps the finished sauce from tasting like flour, and it's worth the two or three minutes it takes.

It's a forgiving dish. Add leftover ham, cooked broccoli, or buffalo chicken sausage before baking, or leave it plain and serve hot sauce on the side. Once you've made it a few times, feel free to treat the ingredient list as a starting point rather than a rule.
